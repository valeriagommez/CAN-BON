A Canadian Biodiversity Observation Network is essential

It will aid in coordinating biodiversity monitoring efforts and providing decision-makers with credible and timely relevant information. CAN BON is a vital layer that will inform decisions and projections in the near and long term.

The 30x30 initiative

Protecting 30% of Canadian lands and waters by 2030

Canada is advancing an ambitious “Force for Nature” strategy, with a commitment of $3.8 billion for the 30x30 initiative.

This is in alignment with the UN Convention on Biological Diversity (CBD) Kunming-Montreal Global Biodiversity Framework (KMGBF).

About CAN BON

Nature is undergoing a major transformation due to climate change. Biodiversity loss is accelerating and with it the many economic and cultural benefits we derive from healthy ecosystems.

At the same time, demand for decision-useful data is increasing and there is a great need to mainstream information to support investment decisions.

Achieving these aforementioned goals requires reliable, coordinated data on the changing state of Canada’s biodiversity.
